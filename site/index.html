1:00 · Prerequisites

Prerequisites: measure baseline latency and errors, and confirm that this endpoint permits data up to thirty seconds old. Never use this recipe for authorization decisions.

2:00 · Rollout

Rollout procedure: shadow reads first, compare the cached and uncached responses, then enable caching for five percent of traffic behind a reversible flag.

4:00 · Rollback

Rollback condition: if the error rate increases by more than one percentage point over baseline, disable the flag and restore uncached reads. Keep monitoring after rollback.
